About KTU CGPA Pro

KTU CGPA Pro is a free tool made for APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University students.

We made this because all other calculators needed marks. We wanted to enter S, A+, A, B grades directly and also plan our target CGPA.

Features:

  • Direct Grade to Grade Point conversion as per KTU
  • Target CGPA Planner
  • Mobile friendly and 100% Free
  • No login, No data collection

Made with ❤️ for KTU students in Kerala
